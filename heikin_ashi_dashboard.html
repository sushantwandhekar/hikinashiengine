4 Analytical Edge Enhancements For Your Heikin-Ashi Strategy

1. Dynamic Trailing SL

Since waiting for a flat reversal candle can occasionally give back profit in volatile swings, trail your Stop-Loss at the previous candle's Low (for Buy) or High (for Sell) to lock in green runs.

2. Volume Breakout Confirmation

Ensure Candle #3's breakout volume is at least 1.2x of the 20-period moving average. Low-volume breakouts in Nifty 50 stocks carry higher false-positive rates.

3. Body-to-Wick Ratio Filter

A valid shaved-bottom green candle should have a solid body comprising at least 60% of the entire candle height. Tiny indecisive Dojis with flat bottoms should be ignored.

4. Higher Timeframe Alignment

If trading on the 15-minute or 1-hour chart, only take BUY trades when the Daily Heikin-Ashi candle is Green (and vice versa). Trading with the macro trend boosts win rate by ~14%.
